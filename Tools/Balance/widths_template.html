Included: catalog prices, recipe quantities and timers, research prerequisites/payments, paid construction and drones, binary power, fuel, shared production, machine buffers and changeovers, phased Bay/Fighter work, reclamation, pooled input-belt and machine-output budgets.

Not yet a complete spatial game player: physical belt geometry, placement effort, combat and arbitrary routing/sell/rebuild strategies are not simulated. The benchmark automatically pauses consumers and moves cargo within its budgets. A width is a generated policy, not proof of the best possible scheduling at that size.

The full canonical grid can be run with --trials 0. The selected sample is deterministic. Raw replays, policy definitions, catalog and source hashes are saved alongside this report. Best results are measured at the shown tick size, not proven global optima.
